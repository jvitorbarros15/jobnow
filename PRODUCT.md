# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Active job seekers in the middle of a stressful, multi-front job search. They're managing 10-30 applications at once, anxious about ghosting, waiting on responses, and trying not to drop anything. They open JobNow to get a clear picture and act fast, not to browse or explore.

## Product Purpose

JobNow is a focused job application tracker that syncs with Gmail to automatically surface and organize applications. Success looks like: a user opens the dashboard, immediately understands where they stand across their whole pipeline, and leaves with their next action clear.

## Brand Personality

Ambitious, trustworthy, precise. The product respects that the user is working toward something serious. It never treats their search as trivial. Reference energy: Stripe Dashboard. Confident and data-rich without being overwhelming.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS (Notion/Airtable clones): cream backgrounds, rounded everything, purple or teal accents, no real opinion. Feels like a template, not a tool.
- Startup pitch deck aesthetic: gradient-heavy, hero metrics on a glow, decorative animations, feels like a demo not a daily driver.
- LinkedIn: corporate, cluttered, dark-pattern-y. The opposite of respecting the user's attention.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity earns trust.** The user is stressed. Every element either reduces that stress or adds to it. Ruthlessly prioritize readable data over visual decoration.
2. **Boldness through precision.** Ambitious doesn't mean loud. Like Stripe, confidence comes from tight spacing, strong type hierarchy, and decisive color — not from effects or gradients.
3. **Show the full picture instantly.** Pipeline visibility is the core value. The user should understand their entire job search status in under five seconds.
4. **Tools don't decorate, they perform.** This is a daily driver, not a first impression. Every interaction should feel fast and reliable, not novel.
5. **Earn each data point.** Only surface what helps the user act. Noise is the enemy of a stressed job seeker.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target. Prioritize strong contrast ratios and legible type sizes — the user is often checking this quickly, possibly under stress.
